---------------------------
TYPE: Advanced Technical Confluence

OPERATIONAL LOGIC:
This robot operates in the Volatility market (commonly R_100 or R_10) using three layers of filtering:
1. Trend: Uses Moving Averages (EMA) to determine the macro direction.
2. Momentum: Uses RSI to avoid entering overbought or oversold areas.
3. Confirmation: Uses MACD to validate movement strength before purchase.

MONEY MANAGEMENT (audit, November 2026):
- At start you choose a stake-factor table, e.g. 0.35, 0.39, 0.80, 1.62, 3.36, 6.98, 14.4, 30.06.
- Each consecutive loss moves one step up the table; a win resets it.
- From step 2 the stake roughly doubles after each loss: this is a CAPPED MARTINGALE (audit grade C).
- The full table above sums to about 58 times the base unit. Make sure your session stop-loss
  is reached before a single losing streak can exceed it.

RECOMMENDED SETTINGS:
- Initial Stake: 0.35 USD
- Target (Take Profit): 10% of bankroll per session
- Stop Loss: 15% of bankroll
- Check: 15% of the bankroll must be larger than the sum of the steps you allow,
  otherwise the stop-loss cannot protect you within one losing streak.
- Test on a demo account first. Recommended for clear trending markets.
